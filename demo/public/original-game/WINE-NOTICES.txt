The original distributor applied patches to Wine 11.0. The main runtime uses
setupapi-detach, Explorer-startup, DirectInput-hook, localhost-file-URI,
WineD3D persistent GDI surface and FAudio OpenAL fixes. The independent PE32
WineD3D/WebGL DLLs use the v42 patch series. Rebuild recipes, exact source pins,
patches and configuration:
https://github.com/danoon2/Boxedwine/tree/509f6a7545e0827a27ed1b4ebab66ac1da4b1ea7/tools/buildWine
https://github.com/danoon2/Boxedwine/blob/509f6a7545e0827a27ed1b4ebab66ac1da4b1ea7/tools/wineTests/webgl-test-divergences-v42.json
https://github.com/danoon2/Boxedwine/tree/509f6a7545e0827a27ed1b4ebab66ac1da4b1ea7/tools/wineTests

codecs, fonts and BusyBox. Preserve their individual notices and obtain source
matching their packaged versions from the original distribution source records:

Gecko, DXVK and Vulkan are omitted from this upstream web archive; they are not
new components redistributed by this runtime publication.

Our modified BoxedWine emulator and gl4es source package is separate from this
unchanged guest filesystem. Its publication provides SOURCE.txt, COPYING,
source-manifest.json and bounded source archive parts beside the runtime.
