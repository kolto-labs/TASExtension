Score

    One note per line: a tick count, then the keys held, or . for none. Lines that start with @ are directives. A # starts a comment. The lit line is the one playing.

    Record writes the pad keys into the score at each new tick. Click a pad key to hold or release it. Your keyboard never sends game input.

    Piano roll

    greenzone restore point wait or load @mark first divergence not played

    Click a cell to toggle it, or drag to paint. Click a tick number to seek. Keys: arrows move, Shift+left or right moves 10, Shift+up or down extends, [ and ] change column, T toggles, Delete clears, N adds a mark, Enter seeks, Shift+F10 opens the menu.
